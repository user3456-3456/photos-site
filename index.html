About this site

Little Things Kept is my photo journal. I started it in my second week of learning to code, as a place to keep the pictures that mean the most to me.

I don't chase perfect shots. I photograph quiet moments: morning light on a wall, a stranger's smile, the street after rain. These are the details we forget first, and the ones we miss the most.

Everything here was built with plain HTML and CSS.

What I look for

  • Light that changes how a place feels
  • Faces caught in the middle of being themselves
  • Textures like rust, cloth, bark and old paint
  • Stillness in the middle of a busy day
